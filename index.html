PHONE = the camera.
iPAD = the screen you watch on.
Open this same page on BOTH.

Do the steps in order:
1 PHONE > 2 PHONE > 3 iPAD > 4 iPAD > 5 PHONE
Then the light turns green: LINKED.
iPAD STEP 3: PASTE THE PHONE CODE
Paste the long code the phone made in step 2.
iPAD STEP 4: SEND REPLY TO PHONE
Send it to the phone, paste it in PHONE STEP 5, tap CONNECT.
